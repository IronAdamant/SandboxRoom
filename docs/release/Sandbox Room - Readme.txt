SANDBOX ROOM - by Deadmansprice
A clean, up-to-date testing facility for Fallout 4, reached by holotape.

GETTING THERE
A few seconds after you get the Pip-Boy (in Vault 111, with an alternative-start mod, or shortly after loading an
existing save), the (Sandbox Room) Holotape is added to your inventory. Play it from the Pip-Boy:
  Teleport In   remembers exactly where you are and takes you to the Sandbox Room
  Teleport Out  takes you back to exactly where you were, with everything you are carrying
Console: coc SandboxRoom (no return point that way).

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Main hall: crafting stations (weapons, armour, chemistry, cooking, robot workbench, Nuka-Mixer, power armour with
  three empty frames) and signed storage cabinets that never run out (Weapons & Ammo, Apparel, Mods, Power Armor,
  Aid, Miscellaneous), with every DLC's items.
- Target range: four walled bays of holographic targets that stand still, never fight back and give no XP.
  Each bay's terminal sets the target, its health and its armour. Shooting galleries at two heights.
- Arena: pick an enemy (base game, Automatron, Far Harbor, Nuka-World), then how many (1-10). Enemies stay frozen
  until you walk in, and freeze again when you leave. They cannot follow you out of the arena. Balconies at two
  heights. Kill or remove everything spawned from the terminals.
- Perks & Stats (new in 1.1, through the door under its sign on the north wall): six terminals.
    S.P.E.C.I.A.L.   raise or lower each attribute, or set all seven at once
    Perks            add or remove any perk chart perk a rank at a time or all ranks at once, remove all perks,
                     or reset them and get the perk points back
    Perk Points      add unspent perk points
    Level            level up by 1 to 50 (each level brings its perk point); levels can't be lowered
    Factions         Minutemen, Brotherhood of Steel, Railroad, Institute: friendly, neutral or hostile; the
                     menu shows where each one stands
    Hostile Groups   raiders, gunners, super mutants, Children of Atom, Triggermen, the Forged, synths,
                     Rust Devils, the Mechanist's robots, trappers and the Nuka-World gangs: friendly or
                     hostile, one at a time or all at once (they attack neutrals on sight, so no neutral)
  Story quests can change faction relations again, and turning a story faction friendly or hostile can break its
  quests: save first.

PLAYING FAIR
Everything in the room is free. Save before you teleport in, and load that save if you don't want to keep what
you took.

INSTALLATION
Mod manager (Vortex, Mod Organizer 2): install the archive. The installer asks which game you have:
  Packed (Next-Gen)       the current game (next-gen update, 1.10.980 or later). Recommended.
  Loose Files (Old-Gen)   the old-gen game (1.10.163), which cannot read the packed archives.
Then enable Sandbox Room.esp.
Manual: copy the contents of ONE folder, "00 Packed (Next-Gen)" or "01 Loose Files (Old-Gen)", into
Fallout 4\Data and enable the plugin.

REQUIREMENTS
Fallout 4, next-gen or old-gen, with all the DLC: Automatron, Far Harbor, Nuka-World, Contraptions Workshop
and Vault-Tec Workshop (Game of the Year / Anniversary editions include them). No F4SE. Light plugin (ESL).

VERSION HISTORY
1.1.0  Perks & Stats room: S.P.E.C.I.A.L., perks, perk points, level and faction relations.
1.0.0  First release.

UNINSTALLING
Teleport out first and use "Remove everything spawned" on the arena terminals. Keep a save from before you
installed the mod.
