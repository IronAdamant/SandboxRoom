SANDBOX ROOM
A clean, up-to-date testing facility for Fallout 4, reached by holotape. PC and Xbox. No F4SE.

WHY THIS EXISTS
I couldn't find a decent, up-to-date sandbox or "cheater's room" that still works. QASmoke is painful to use (that sky...), and the similar mods I tried were either abandoned or broken by the next-gen update. So I made my own: one interior facility with everything you need to test weapons, armour, builds and enemies, and nothing that touches the rest of your game.

GETTING THERE
A few seconds after you get the Pip-Boy (in Vault 111, with an alternative start, or shortly after loading an existing save), the (Sandbox Room) Holotape is added to your inventory. Play it from the Pip-Boy:
- Teleport In: remembers exactly where you are and takes you to the Sandbox Room.
- Teleport Out: takes you back to exactly where you were, with everything you are carrying.
- Safety net (new in 1.1): turn it on or off (it starts off). While it is on you can't die anywhere: going down brings you to the Sandbox Room, healed (health, limbs and radiation), once you are back on your feet, and Teleport Out takes you back to where you fell.
Your companions, Dogmeat included, come with you on Teleport In, Teleport Out and the safety net. (The game brings one you told to wait along too.)
PC console alternative: coc SandboxRoom (no return point that way).

THE MAIN HALL
A three-storey lab in a clean white high-tech finish, with a proper ceiling and lighting. No skybox in sight.
- Crafting stations: weapons, armour, chemistry, cooking, the Automatron robot workbench, the Nuka-World Nuka-Mixer, and a power armour station with three empty power armour frames beside it.
- Storage that never runs out: signed sections of lab cabinets. Whatever you take is put straight back.
  - Weapons & Ammo: Ballistic Guns, Energy Weapons, Heavy Weapons, Melee Weapons, Explosives, Ammo (every ammo type, the DLC's included)
  - Apparel: Armor, Clothing, Headwear, Dogmeat Gear
  - Mods: Weapon Mods, Armor Mods
  - Power Armor: Power Armor, Power Armor Mods (next to the power armour station)
  - Aid: Medicine and Chems, Food, Drinks
  - Miscellaneous: Junk (and caps), Magazines, Bobbleheads
  - Every section also has labelled cabinets for each DLC's items: Automatron, Far Harbor, Nuka-World, Contraptions and Vault-Tec.

TARGET RANGE
A firing lane facing four bays walled off from each other, floor to ceiling.
- Holographic targets: blue hologram humans, super mutants and deathclaws that stand still, never fight back and give no XP.
- Bays: Single target, Group of five (for area damage), Legendary target, and practice target stands.
- Each bay's terminal sets the target type, its health (game default, 100, 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000) and its armour (game default, or none so health equals exactly the damage it takes).
- Killed targets come back a few seconds later, just beside where they stood.
- Shooting galleries at two heights over the lane, with stairs, to test from above.

THE ARENA
Through the door under the ARENA sign.
- A lobby of spawn terminals: Base Game, Automatron, Far Harbor and Nuka-World.
- Pick a group (Humans, Synths and Robots, Super Mutants, Creatures, Legendaries), pick an enemy, then pick how many: 1 to 10. Each lands at a random spot in the arena.
- A large open space with rocks, mounds and dead trees, no buildings, and railed balconies at two heights to shoot from.
- Enemies stay frozen while you are outside the arena and wake up the moment you walk in, and freeze again when you leave. They can't follow you out of the arena: step back into the lobby to catch your breath.
- Rounds (new in 1.1): you can't die in the arena. Going down ends the round: the enemies still standing freeze, healed, back at their spawn points, and you are healed. Once you are up, a message tells you which knockout it was and how long you lasted, and the next round starts 13 seconds later. Walk out into the lobby to stop.
- "Kill everything spawned" and "Remove everything spawned" clean up.

PERKS & STATS (new in 1.1)
Through the door under the PERKS & STATS sign on the north wall: six terminals in a row.
- S.P.E.C.I.A.L.: raise or lower each attribute by one, set it to 1, 5 or 10, or set all seven at once.
- Perks: every perk chart perk, by S.P.E.C.I.A.L.: add or remove a rank, add all ranks at once, or remove them. "Remove all perks", or Reset to remove them all and get the perk points back.
- Perk Points: add unspent points (1, 5, 10, 25).
- Level: level up by 1, 5, 10, 25 or 50; each level brings its perk point. (Levels can't be lowered without F4SE.)
- Factions: Minutemen, Brotherhood of Steel, Railroad, Institute: friendly, neutral or hostile. The menu shows where each one stands.
- Hostile Groups: raiders, gunners, super mutants, Children of Atom, Triggermen, the Forged, synths, Rust Devils, the Mechanist's robots, trappers, Disciples, Operators and the Pack: friendly or hostile, one at a time or all at once. (They attack neutrals on sight, so there is no neutral.)
- Story quests can change faction relations again, and turning a story faction friendly or hostile can break its quests: save first.

HACK AND LOCK PRACTICE (new in 1.1)
Through the door under its sign on the west wall, right behind you when you arrive.
- Footlockers locked Novice, Advanced, Expert and Master, and terminals locked the same, all labelled.
- Pick or hack one and it locks itself again a few seconds after you step away: practise as often as you like.
- A footlocker of bobby pins that never runs out.
- Advanced and above need the Locksmith or Hacker perk, as anywhere else in the game: the Perks terminal next door can add it.

Doors open by themselves as you walk up to them. Enemies can follow you up the stairs.

PLAYING FAIR
Everything in the room is free. Whether you keep it is up to you: save before you teleport in, and load that save if you don't want to keep what you took.

REQUIREMENTS
- Fallout 4 with all the DLC: Automatron, Far Harbor, Nuka-World, Contraptions Workshop and Vault-Tec Workshop. (The Game of the Year and Anniversary editions include them all. Wasteland Workshop is not needed.)
- No F4SE needed.
- A light plugin: it doesn't use up a load order slot.

COMPATIBILITY
- Everything happens in its own new interior cell. The mod does not edit any vanilla records, except the navmesh info map that every mod adding a navmesh has to touch.
- Load order: anywhere.
- Safe to add to an existing save.

CHANGELOG

- New: Perks & Stats room: S.P.E.C.I.A.L., perks, perk points, level and faction relations. The faction menus show where each faction stands.
- New: Hack and Lock Practice room: locks and terminals, Novice to Master, that lock themselves again after every try, and a never-empty bobby pin chest.

- New: safety net on the holotape (off by default): going down anywhere brings you to the room, healed.

1.0.0
- First release. (An Xbox update to 1.0.0 fixed the signs showing pink.)

UNINSTALLING
Turn the safety net off (holotape), teleport out, and use "Remove everything spawned" on the arena terminals if you spawned anything. Then remove the mod. As with any scripted mod, uninstalling mid-playthrough is at your own risk: keep a save from before you installed it.

PLAYSTATION
Sorry: I cannot upload a version that works. This mod relies on its own scripts (refilling cabinets, doors, spawn terminals, the arena, the target bays, the Perks & Stats terminals, the relocking practice locks, the holotape and its safety net) and its own sign assets, and PlayStation mods can't include either. A PlayStation version would just be an empty building.

KNOWN NOTES
- Legendary targets in the target range are the game's own legendary enemies, so they do give XP.
- Enemies you spawn in the arena stay until you kill or remove them.
