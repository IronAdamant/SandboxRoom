A clean, up-to-date testing facility, reached by holotape. No skybox, no F4SE. Requires all DLC.

A few seconds after you get the Pip-Boy (alternative starts too), the (Sandbox Room) Holotape is added. Teleport In takes you to the room; Teleport Out returns you where you were.

- Crafting stations incl. robot workbench and Nuka-Mixer, plus a power armour station with 3 frames.
- Signed storage cabinets that never run out, base game and every DLC: Weapons & Ammo, Apparel, Mods, Power Armor, Aid, Misc.
- Target range: holographic targets that stand still, never fight back and give no XP. Set health (up to 100,000) and armour. Galleries at two heights.
- Arena: base game, Automatron, Far Harbor and Nuka-World enemies - pick one and how many (1-10). They stay frozen until you walk in.

Playing fair is up to you: save before teleporting in.

Own interior cell, no vanilla records edited. Light plugin.
