# Sandbox Room changelog

## 1.1.0

New
- **Perks & Stats room** (north wall): S.P.E.C.I.A.L., perks (add, remove, reset with the points back), perk
  points, level, and faction relations for the story factions and the hostile groups. The faction menus show where
  each faction stands and offer only the other relations.
- **Hack and Lock Practice room** (west wall): footlockers and terminals locked Novice, Advanced, Expert and Master
  that lock themselves again after every try, and a bobby pin footlocker that never runs out.

Fixes
- Xbox: the signs no longer show pink (the Xbox archives now come from the Creation Kit, which tiles the textures
  for the console).
- Lighting: characters are lit properly in the room. Its ambient light came equally from every side, which
  flattened faces and hid face-lighting mods.
- Arena: "Brotherhood of Steel (hostile)" and "Institute (hostile)" now spawn (the game's lists spawn nothing until
  the story turns those factions hostile) and always fight you, whatever the Factions terminal says.
- Weapon cabinets: no more stray ammo (the game slips a few rounds in with every weapon in a container). All ammo,
  the DLC's included, is in the Ammo cabinet, and the DLC weapon cabinets are now just "Weapons".
- Removed NPC and quest weapons from the cabinets: the Contraptions firework mortar's shells, a quest nuke, the
  Suicider's mini nuke, Nuka-World's turret alien blaster, and Creation Club VR placeholders.
